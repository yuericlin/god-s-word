# Privacy Policy for God's Word

Effective date: October 28, 2026

God's Word ("the app") is a Bible reader. This policy explains what data the
app handles and what it does not.

## Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no
developer-run servers. The developer never receives, collects, sells, or
shares your personal data, and notes and bookmarks are never sent to the
developer.

## Data stored on your device

Your data — installed Bibles, reading positions, bookmarks, notes, reading
history, and settings such as text size — is stored on your iPhone.

## Optional sync and backup, under your control

- **iCloud:** If you turn on iCloud sync, your data is stored in your own
  iCloud Drive account, provided by Apple.
- **Google Drive:** Backup is off until you sign in. It uses the
  `drive.appdata` scope only, and backups are written to your own Google
  Drive.

The developer cannot access data stored in your iCloud or Google Drive
accounts.

## Downloads

Bible texts and related modules download directly from the repository you
choose (by default, CrossWire's public repository) to your device. As with
any internet download, the server you download from can see your device's
IP address; the app adds no tracking of its own.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with
a new effective date.
